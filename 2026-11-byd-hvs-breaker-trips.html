BYD HVS+ on Fronius GEN24: breaker trips traced to BMS firmware 1.28

#fronius #solar #byd

This article documents how recurring breaker trips of a BYD Battery-Box HVS+ on a Fronius GEN24 inverter were traced to the Battery Management System (BMS) firmware V1.28.0. In addition, it describes the firmware update over Bluetooth. The fix is not yet confirmed.

Symptom

The test system consists of a Fronius Symo GEN24 6.0 SC Plus inverter and a BYD Battery-Box HVS+ with 5 modules.

  • The breaker on the Battery Control Unit (BCU) has dropped approximately once a week since late October. Each trip required a manual reset.
  • The trips occur at low power, without load peaks. The Residual Current Device (RCD) is not involved.

Findings

  • (F1) Three events cleared by themselves within 7 minutes. The other four lasted until a manual reset.
  • (F2) Five of the seven events began during standby (code 1175, "Too low DC power for feed-in").
  • (F3) On September 10, 30 minutes before the first dropout, the log shows BYD3-48 "Pre-charge Fault - Fault2". It is the only BYD code in the log.
  • (F4) The dropouts occur with the minimum State of Charge (SOC) at 7% and at 50%. Therefore, the SOC level is not the trigger.
  • (F5) The BYD Energy app shows no alarms, a State of Health (SOH) of 100%, 89 cycles, and a cell spread of 16 mV (3316 to 3332 mV).
  • (F6) The BMS firmware as found was V1.28.0.

Cause

  • (C1) The BMS still ran the delivery firmware V1.28.0 (F6).
  • (C2) BYD requires BMS 1.29 or newer for Fronius inverters, in addition to GEN24 firmware 1.39.5-1 or newer and the "Compatible Battery" setting (notice of November 20, 2025).
  • (C3) A known defect causes sporadic pre-charge failures (EC108) on HVB, HVS+, and HVM+, mostly with Fronius inverters (compare F3). According to EFT, BMS V1.33 targets it (forum).
  • (C4) The dropouts cluster in energy saving mode, i.e., at minimum SOC and without photovoltaic (PV) power (compare F2). The system has entered this mode almost nightly since mid-October (Fronius manual).

Firmware update

The firmware was updated over Bluetooth on November 14, 2026. The update of all three components took approximately one hour.

The update comprises the following steps.

  1. Open the BYD Energy app in local mode, without an account, and connect to the battery. Pitfalls (P2) to (P4) apply.
  2. Select Setting > Firmware Upgrade > Firmware Upgrade (Bluetooth) (guide).
  3. Reconnect after each upgrade, as Bluetooth disconnects.
  4. Afterward, restart in this order: inverter off, battery off, battery on, inverter on.

Pitfalls

  • (P1) The lit button on the BCU is the power button. Held for 5 seconds, it shuts the battery down and the breaker drops. This is by design and not a fault.
  • (P2) The pairing button sits on the Smart WiFi/LAN module. It must be held for approximately 7 seconds until the blue LED blinks fast. Holding it for more than 10 seconds triggers a factory reset.
  • (P3) The battery must be added by the BCU serial, which equals the Bluetooth name of the module. With any other serial, the app never connects.
  • (P4) The module rejects pairing in the Android Bluetooth settings. The connection must be made inside the app, with Bluetooth and location switched on.
  • (P5) The Solar.web codes 995, 996, and 999 are portal data gaps, not battery faults (Fronius Solar.web support).

Status

  • The fix is unconfirmed. The criterion is two weeks without code 1240.
  • Two checks remain open (C2): the GEN24 firmware at 1.39.5-1 or newer, and the battery configured as "Compatible Battery".
  • If the fault recurs, the next steps are (1) a check of the DC cabling between battery and inverter and (2) a BCU replacement under warranty.
